I pooled the ratings of all 251,137 active men and 30,420 active women into one empirical distribution, then ran a Monte Carlo simulation of the top order statistics: draw both groups from the same pool, at the headcounts FIDE actually has, and see how far apart the two top 100s land. Half the gap follows from headcount alone. The other half is a difference between the two rating distributions, and this data cannot say what causes it. Full project.
